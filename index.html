Smart Irrigation System

Water the crop when it needs it, not when someone remembers.

An Arduino based controller that reads soil moisture, temperature and light, learns when the farmer usually waters each bed, and then runs the pumps on its own while keeping an eye on plant health.

  • Learns watering times from the farmer's manual use
  • Two pump zones with dry-run and timeout protection
  • Plant health score and crop check for every zone
  • Works with real sensors or in demo mode
Mini project · Arduino UNO + HTML / CSS / JavaScript
